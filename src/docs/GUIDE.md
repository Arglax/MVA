# MVA player guide

MVA is AdventureQuest Worlds on your phone, with bigger buttons and a few extras for a small screen. Everything here can be switched off.

## The Sidebar

These are the game's own menus, made big enough to tap: Quests, Map, Bag, Bank and the rest.

1. **Drops** holds the loot waiting for you, and shows a red number when something dropped.
2. **Quick** opens shortcuts and travel.
3. **MVA crest** in the corner opens MVA's own Settings, Graphics, this guide and About.

> **Note:** To hide the side buttons, turn off **Sidebar** in the game's Options. A small MVA crest stays in the corner.

## Turning MVA's changes on and off

Open the game's **Options**, then **Controls**. Every row with a gold **MVA** tag is one of ours. Tap a row to switch it, or tap **(?)** to see what it does.

1. **MVA UI** is the main switch: turn it off and you get the game exactly as it normally looks.

Most of these switches are also in the crest's **Settings**.

> **Note:** **Bigger UI** and **Long Dash** need a restart to take effect.

| Switch | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **MVA UI** | The main switch for everything below. |
| **Sidebar** | Big menu buttons on both sides. |
| **Bigger UI** | Taller list rows. |
| **Mobile Layout** | Skill buttons around the attack button, bigger portraits. |
| **Dynamic Joystick** | Walk with your thumb anywhere on the left side. |
| **Long Dash** | The dash goes three times as far. |
| **Scrolling** | Swipe lists, shops, quests and the chat. |
| **Inventory** | Bigger bag rows with class ranks and enhancements. |
| **Inventory Filter** | A Sort button in the bag. |
| **Star Rating** | Star ratings on classes. |
| **Improved Auras** | The aura windows. |
| **Drops** | Loot waits in the Drops panel. |
| **Improve FPS** | Smoother, steadier movement. |

## Playing with touch (MFUI)

1. Set AQW's **Mobile Controls** option to **On**. It is in the game's Options, under Controls.
2. Set MVA's **Mobile Layout** to **On** to put the skill buttons around the attack button.

- **Dynamic Joystick** lets you put your thumb anywhere on the left side to walk.
- **Long Dash** makes the dash go three times as far.
- **Scrolling** lets you swipe through lists, shops, quests and the chat.

## Bag

Taller rows that are easier to tap.

- Classes show their **rank** and a **star rating**.
- Gear shows its **enhancement** and **boosts**.

To sort your gear:

1. Open the **Bag**.
2. Tap **Sort** next to the title.
3. Pick a damage type or a boost.

> **Note:** The stars come from community guides. Where no guide rates a class, they are an estimate. Use them as a starting point, not a rule.

## Auras (buffs and debuffs)

Two small windows: **yours** on the left, your **target's** at the top right. Each line shows the aura, its stacks, the time left and who applied it.

- **Red** blocks healing, like Decay.
- **Orange** is a taunt or enrage.
- **Blue glow** means it is at its maximum.
- **3/5** means 3 stacks out of a maximum of 5.

Tap a line to see more. Drag a window by its title to move it.

- **–** shrinks the window, **×** hides it.
- **⇆** joins the two windows, or splits them again.
- **A** changes the text size, **⚙** has pins and filters.

> **Note:** To bring back a hidden window, tap a portrait, then **Show Auras**.

## Quick: shortcuts and travel

1. Tap **Quick** on the right side.
2. Pick a tab: **Actions** or **Travel**.

- **Actions**: one-tap shortcuts. See who is in the room, show HP bars, hide other players, rest, open the chat.
- **Travel**: go to a map without typing `/join`. Pick a place, type a map name, or tap one in your **travel log**.
- Add a number for a private room, like `battleon-1234`.

> **Note:** The game makes you wait 5 seconds between maps.

## Drops

Loot waits in the Drops panel instead of popping up in the middle of a fight.

1. Tap **Drops** on the right side.
2. Tap **Accept** or **Decline** on each item.

## Performance Issues

1. Tap the **MVA crest**, then **Graphics**.
2. Pick **Battery** or **Balanced**.

Also useful, in the crest's **Settings**:

- **Auto quality** lowers the graphics only while the game struggles.
- **Show FPS** shows how smoothly the game runs. 30 or more is good.
- **Headless mode** turns the picture off but keeps you logged in. Tap the screen to bring it back.

## Back button

- A window is open: **Back** closes it.
- Nothing is open: **Back** sends the game to the background. You stay connected.

## Privacy

MVA sends anonymous usage numbers, such as phone model, frame rate and which buttons get used. Never your name, login or chat.

To turn that off:

1. Tap the **MVA crest**, then **Settings**.
2. Switch off **Send anonymous usage stats**.

> **Note:** If MVA crashes, it asks before sending a report. **Yes** sends the full report with your phone's details. **No** sends only the error.
